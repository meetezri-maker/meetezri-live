Welcome to Talk To Solace Early Access

Hi,

Thank you for being one of the first people invited to experience Solace.

As one of our first 31 Alpha Testers, you are getting access to Solace at a very early stage of development. This means you'll be among the first people helping us shape the future of the platform through your feedback and experiences.

Because this is an alpha release, you may occasionally encounter features that are still being refined. Our AI avatars, conversations, and platform features are continuously being enhanced, and your feedback will help us fine-tune and elevate the overall experience as we introduce future improvements and capabilities.

Rather than evaluating Solace as a finished product, we invite you to explore it as an early build and help us identify opportunities for improvement. Your feedback will play a direct role in helping us refine and enhance the experience before broader release.

Over the past several months, we've been building Solace with a simple goal: to create a space where people can pause, reflect, understand themselves better, and build healthier mental wellness habits over time.

Today, we're opening early access to a select group of members, and we're excited to have you with us.

This is the first phase of Solace. Additional features, improvements, and experiences are already planned as we continue to grow and evolve the platform. Your experience during this early access period will help us learn, improve, and continue building something meaningful.

Areas We'd Love You to Explore

• Home — Your personalized dashboard with an overview of your wellness journey, recent activity, and recommended next steps.

• Talk It Out — Connect with the Solace AI avatar for supportive, judgment-free conversations that help you process thoughts, emotions, and everyday challenges.

• Mood Tracking — Log and monitor your moods over time to identify patterns and gain deeper self-awareness.

• Journal — Capture reflections, experiences, and personal insights in a private space for self-expression.

• Habit Tracker — Build and maintain healthy routines by tracking habits that support your well-being.

• Sleep Tracker — Record and review your sleep patterns to better understand and improve your rest.

• Brain Health — Explore insights and assessments that help you better understand your mental wellness.

• Progress — View your growth, milestones, and wellness trends over time.

• Wellness Tools — Access mindfulness exercises, coping strategies, and resources designed to support your mental health.

• Community — Connect with others, share experiences, share your achievements, and engage in a supportive wellness-focused environment.

• Achievements — Celebrate milestones and accomplishments as you continue building positive wellness habits.

To Get the Most Meaningful Experience

We recommend:

• Using Talk It Out multiple times during the access period.

• Checking in with your mood regularly.

• Creating journal entries across multiple days.

• Exploring wellness exercises and mindfulness activities.

• Reviewing your Brain Health insights several times.

• Tracking habits, sleep, and progress over time.

• Exploring each section of the platform at least once.
